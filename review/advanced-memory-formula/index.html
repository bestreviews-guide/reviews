Does It Work? What the Evidence Actually Says

Here's where I have to be straight with you. There is real research on these ingredients. There is no real research on this product. Those are two very different things.

The ingredients themselves are genuinely well studied. Bacopa has trials showing modest improvements in memory recall after 8–12 weeks. Ginkgo has decades of research on blood flow, with mixed results on memory itself. Phosphatidylserine has trials in older adults showing small gains in recall. NeuroFactor has a published study showing it raises BDNF levels in blood — one 100 mg dose raised plasma BDNF by 143% in ten people, though no memory outcome was measured. That's a respectable evidence base for the ingredients — better than most brain pills I review.
