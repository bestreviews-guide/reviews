Advanced Mitochondrial Formula Reviews

That is my full review, with the label checked line by line. The formula comes from Advanced Bionutritionals, is made with Dr. Frank Shallenberger, and holds eleven ingredients in two capsules a day. One bottle holds 60 capsules, a 30-day supply.

The dose gap is the heart of it. CoQ10 is 50 mg in the bottle, against 100-300 mg in the trials. PQQ is 5 mg, against 20 mg in the studies. D-ribose is 250 mg, against 15 grams a day in the research. Niacinamide is 200 mg, acetyl-L-carnitine is 190 mg, and curcumin is 180 mg. The names are right, the amounts are small.

The price is premium. One bottle costs $79.95, which is $2.67 a day. Three bottles cost $214.95, and six cost $399.90, with free shipping on the larger packs. There is no autoship, and payment is one-off.

The guarantee is the best part of the offer. It runs for 90 days, down to the last pill, and you can return the bottles even if they are empty, for a full refund. Shipping is covered too. That matters, because the makers themselves say to give a mitochondrial formula at least three months.

Who should buy it: someone whose energy has dropped and who likes an eleven-ingredient label they can actually read, with a 90-day guarantee behind it. Who should skip it: anyone who wants the doses the studies used. Anyone on blood pressure, blood sugar or heart medication should ask their own doctor first. This is general information only, not medical advice.

My take: buy it for the ingredient list and the guarantee, not for the promise of feeling decades younger. The label is honest, the doses are small, and the guarantee takes the risk out of trying it for three months.
