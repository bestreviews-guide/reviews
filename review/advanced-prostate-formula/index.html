What Does the Evidence Say?

The ingredients have research behind them; the product itself doesn't — and the most famous ingredient failed its biggest test. Saw palmetto helps block the conversion of testosterone into DHT, but the largest, best-designed trial — a 2011 JAMA study — found it no better than placebo, even at triple the standard dose.

Beta-sitosterol is the strongest ingredient here: trials using 60–130 mg a day improved urinary flow and reduced residual urine volume in men with BPH. But this label holds 50 mg — just under the trial range. DIM has lab and animal research but no large human trial for prostate symptoms.
