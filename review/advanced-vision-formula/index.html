What Is Advanced Vision Formula?

Advanced Vision Formula is a daily eye tablet from Advanced Bionutritionals, formulated by Dr. Frank Shallenberger, sold for screen fatigue, glare and age-related vision worry. It's sold only through the official site, and it's made in the USA — the label lists the company address as Norcross, Georgia.

Does It Work? What the Evidence Says

The short version: the ingredients have research behind them, the product itself doesn't. The AREDS2 trial from 2013 — over 4,000 participants — tested 10 mg of lutein and 2 mg of zeaxanthin a day and found they reduced progression to advanced macular degeneration in people at high risk. The label confirms the doses: 10 mg of lutein matches AREDS2 exactly, and 3 mg of zeaxanthin beats the 2 mg AREDS2 used.

Vitamin A is essential for night vision. Citicoline has small trials for optic nerve function, and astaxanthin has small trials for eye fatigue.

But no trial has tested this 15-nutrient blend as a whole, and the sales page publishes no amounts. "Reverse the damage caused by screen exposure" is not a fair description of it.

How to Take It

Two tablets a day with water, preferably with meals — 60 tablets, 30 servings, exactly a month.

Taking them with meals matters: lutein, zeaxanthin, astaxanthin and vitamin A are all fat-soluble, so they absorb far better with food than on an empty stomach.

Don't expect obvious change in the first few weeks. The AREDS2 benefits were measured over years, not weeks.
