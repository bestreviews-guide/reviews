How Does It Work? What Does the Evidence Say?

The idea is straightforward: support hyaluronan, and the synovial fluid stays thicker and more cushioning. Mobilee is the centrepiece — a patented rooster-comb extract. A small pilot study of Mobilee, published in Nutrición Hospitalaria, reported better joint comfort, but it tested Mobilee alone, not Joint Genesis.

The supporting ingredients have their own, older evidence. Boswellia was tested in a 2003 trial by Kimmatkar and colleagues (PMID 12622457) at 100–300 mg, and ginger in a 2001 trial by Altman and Marcussen (PMID 11564676) at 250 mg to 1 g. Both showed some promise, but the trials were small, and Joint Genesis doesn’t disclose its boswellia or ginger doses.

There is no published trial of Joint Genesis itself, and no evidence it can regrow cartilage or cure osteoarthritis.
