What Is Joseph's Well?

Joseph's Well is a digital DIY blueprint program that teaches you, step by step, how to build your own atmospheric water generator — a machine that makes drinking water from the air. The idea is simple to describe: cool humid air below its dew point, collect the condensate, and filter it. It's the same principle behind a dehumidifier, put to work as a water source.

One thing to get out of the way early, because it shapes this whole review: this is a guide, not a water machine. Nothing in the package is a finished generator. You source the parts yourself and build it yourself. The creator is named as John Gilmore, who the sales page itself says is a pen name — an off-grid living enthusiast who created the program. The official pages are at uswaterrevolution.com.

That distinction matters. If you want a pre-built machine shipped to your door, this isn't it. If you like a hands-on project and want to understand — and own — a backup water source, that's the audience this guide is written for.

Safety: What to Check Before You Start

This is a DIY water project, so the safety section is not boilerplate — it is the section. Water you make yourself is only as safe as the way you collect, filter, store and test it.

  • Test the water before you drink it. Condensate can pick up contaminants from the air, from the build materials and from storage. Filter it and get it tested, and follow the program's own guidance on treatment.
  • Check local rules. Water collection, storage and treatment can be regulated where you live. Check your local requirements before you build.
  • Store it properly. Stored water grows things. Use clean, food-grade containers, keep them out of sunlight, and do not store water longer than you should.
  • Do not rely on it as your only supply. This is a backup or supplemental source. Keep your mains supply and your stored water as the primary source.

If you are not comfortable with basic water safety — filtration, testing, clean storage — get professional guidance before you drink anything the system produces.
