The Smoothie Diet Reviews: I Read the Label So You Don't Have To

By Dr. Evans, Independent Supplement Reviewer. Every label read, every dose checked against the studies.

My verdict is 3.6 out of 5.

I have read a lot of diet plans over the years, and The Smoothie Diet Reviews is one I kept coming back to, because the label is actually pretty clear about what you are getting.

Here is what it is: a $27 one-time digital 21-day meal-replacement plan from Health Coach Drew. You get 36+ smoothie recipes, weekly shopping lists, a meal-prep guide, and two bonuses, a 3-Day Detox and a Quick-Start Guide. There is a 60-day money-back refund by email if it does not work out for you.

What I liked is that the plan leans on real evidence rather than hype. The review cites Astbury et al. 2019 and Heymsfield et al. 2003 on meal replacements, which is more grounding than most plans in this price range bother with.

My honest take? You buy blind, as there is no trial of the program itself, so you are trusting the outline before you have tried a single recipe. But at $27 for a full 21-day plan, it is cheap, and the 60-day refund takes some of the sting out of that.
