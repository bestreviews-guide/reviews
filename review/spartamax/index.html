Does SpartaMax Work? What the Evidence Says

Here's the honest split: the ingredients have research behind them, the product itself doesn't — and here the doses are the problem, most of them too small to match the studies.

The strongest ingredient evidence is for tongkat ali. A 2022 systematic review and meta-analysis pooled five randomized trials (232 men) and found tongkat ali raised total testosterone, with the clearest effect in men with low levels — though the trials were small and mostly used one commercial extract (Leisegang et al., 2022, PMID 36013514). Ashwagandha has a 2015 randomized trial showing it improved muscle strength and raised testosterone in men doing resistance training (Wankhede et al., 2015, PMID 26609282). L-arginine has a 2019 meta-analysis finding benefit for erectile dysfunction — but at 1,500 to 5,000 mg a day, thirty to a hundred times what's in this gummy (Rhim et al., 2019, PMID 30879084).

So here's the honest version: a daily gummy with one properly dosed ingredient, six underdosed ones, and a claim that can't be true. There is no published, independent clinical trial of SpartaMax itself, and no supplement has ever been shown to increase penis size.

In weeks 4 to 8, the tongkat ali and ashwagandha studies measured small changes in testosterone and stress markers around this point, so if anything shifts, it's subtle: a bit more drive, a bit steadier mood. In months 3 to 6, the window the 365-day guarantee covers many times over, you'd expect to notice it looking back — if the formula is doing anything for stamina and drive. Safety & Side Effects

For most healthy adults, these are familiar botanicals — maca, ashwagandha, beetroot and grape seed are all things you'd find in food and standard supplements. No stimulants, nothing habit-forming. The side effects that come up most with this kind of blend are mild digestive discomfort in the first few days, and ashwagandha can make some people drowsy.

The cautions that actually matter: L-arginine and beetroot support nitric oxide, which relaxes blood vessels, so if you take blood pressure medication, nitrates or erectile dysfunction drugs, talk to your doctor first — combining vasodilators can drop blood pressure further than you want. Tongkat ali and ashwagandha affect hormone pathways, so if you take hormone therapy, thyroid medication or antidepressants, ask your clinician before starting. Ashwagandha can make some people sleepy and can interact with sedatives, so don't take it with alcohol or sleep medication without asking. Talk to your doctor or pharmacist before starting, especially if you take any medication, and if you notice a new symptom after starting any supplement, stop and get medical advice.
