Safety: What to Check Before You Start

This is a woodworking product, so the safety section is about the workshop, not the body. Plans don't cut timber — you do — and a bad plan can put you in front of a saw with wrong measurements and a false sense of confidence. Check every plan before you cut: read the whole plan, check the measurements against each other, and check the cutting list adds up. Don't build from a scan you can't read — if the drawing is low-resolution or the measurements are vague, skip that plan. And workshop safety is yours, not the plan's: eye and ear protection, sharp blades, no loose clothing near spinning tools, and a clear floor. No plan in this library teaches that. If you're a complete beginner, pair the library with a basic woodworking safety course or a knowledgeable friend for your first builds. The plans give you shapes. They don't give you judgement, and judgement is the part that keeps your fingers attached.
