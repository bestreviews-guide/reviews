What to Know Before You Buy

This is a book, not a supplement, so the safety questions are about food — and they matter more than the price. Canning safety: low-acid foods canned at home carry a botulism risk if the method is wrong, and the book itself warns about this. Foraging: the book covers wild-growing foods, but never eat a wild plant on the basis of one photo — confirm the identification with a second source. Allergies: check every recipe's ingredients before you try it, especially if anyone in your household has a food allergy. Storage: "lasts for years" depends on cool, dry, pest-free storage — a damp basement or a hot attic will shorten shelf life a lot.
